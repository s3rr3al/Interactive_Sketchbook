About Mark Chavez






Originally hailing from a small town in North Orange County, California called Cypress. As a young boy, he was heavily influenced by his older brothers when it came to all things culture(music, art, and extreme sports). He received his first skateboard for Xmas at the age of 11, a cheap knockoff from Big5 Sporting goods. Soon after, he was begging his mother to buy him his first "real" skateboard. After many chores and schoolwork had been completed, he earned a complete setup, it was a Blind original reaper logo graphic, with Venture trucks and Blind brand skateboard wheels. It was this board, that the pre-teen felt a sense of initiation into what was hip at the time. Throughout his teenage experience, he would meet up with friends at the local skateshop, Furnace. From there they would mash down the street, trying to impress one another with their skills on a skateboard. Art as a form of expression was engrained in his DNA before he could even speak. Being influenced by his grandfather's oil paintings and ink illustrations, he couldn't help but be infatuated with skateboard graphic art. It was like no other form of Art he had ever seen. There was chaos, there was teenage humor and adult humor, and plenty of satire (World Industries, Shorty's, and Blind skateboard companies were some of his faves). In high school, he learned about graffiti and graphic design through friends. Finding inspiration from skateboard graphic artists like Bigfoot One, Jeremy Fish, Jim Phillips, and so many more, he started to doodle/sketch during the boring lectures in high school. At the age of 16, he watched a VH1 documentary about the hippie culture of the 1960s and the film 'What The Bleep Do We Know?', soon after he became infatuated with alternate realities and how artist portray these in their artwork. From there, he went down the rabbit hole of optical illusions, pyschedelic & surreal styled art to develop his own take on the categories. He is currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Visual Communnication at San Francisco State University. When he is not studying/working, he enjoys spending his time researching, hiking, and surfing.




His most recent works can be found at: https://serrrreal.threadless.com/
