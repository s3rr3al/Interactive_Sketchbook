Process Reflection

Using all four different types of transforms was fun and challenging. The most challenging part was figuring out the dimensions, but after plenty of trial and error, I was able to find the positions that worked for me. Finding the positions was the easiest part, because there was no predetermined location for any of the elements, which led to more experimentation and more fun. Combining rotate, translate, and skew was confusing, because I thought they all belonged on the same line. It took me a while to figure out that rotate has its own line. Using absolute positioning helped establish a sense of emphasis and bring more awareness towards the scale between the moon and star elements.
